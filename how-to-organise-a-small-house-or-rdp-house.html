How To Organise A Small House Or RDP House

A small house can look big and beautiful if you organise it right. You don't need a big house - you need smart storage. Here is how Mzansi families make a small RDP house feel spacious.

1. The Golden Rule: Everything Must Have A Place

If your things don't have a place, your house will always look messy. Give every item a home. Shoes in one box by the door. Keys on one hook. Remote on one table. When you finish using something, put it back immediately. This one habit changes everything.

2. Use The Walls, Not The Floor

In a small house, floor space is gold. Put shelves high up on the wall for things you don't use every day. Hang pots and pans on hooks in the kitchen. Use nails and string to hang bags, hats and cleaning tools behind the door. The more you put on walls, the bigger your floor looks.

3. Boxes Are Your Best Friend

Don't buy expensive storage. Use ice-cream tubs, 5-litre buckets, and old boxes. Label them with paper: "Winter Clothes", "School Books", "Cables", "Medicine". Put boxes under the bed and on top of cupboards. One big box for toys - when kids are done playing, everything goes in ONE box.

4. One In, One Out Rule

When you buy something new, throw out or give away one old thing. If you buy a new shirt, give away an old one. If you buy a new pot, throw away the broken one. This stops your house from getting full of stuff you don't need.

5. Make Your RDP House Look Bigger

Use light colours - white, light green, cream. Dark colours make a room look small. Keep curtains short and light. Use a big mirror if you have one - it makes a room look double size. Keep the floor clear - don't leave bags and boxes on the floor.

Cheap Storage Ideas: 2-litre bottles cut in half = holders for pens, toothbrushes. Old shoe organiser hung on door = storage for spices, medicine, small items. Plastic crates from Pep = stackable cupboards.

Daily 5-Minute Tidy

Every night, set a timer for 5 minutes. Everyone in the house picks up 10 things and puts them back. In 5 minutes, your whole house will be tidy again. Do this every night and you will never have a big messy clean-up again.

Clean home. Healthy family. Small budget.
