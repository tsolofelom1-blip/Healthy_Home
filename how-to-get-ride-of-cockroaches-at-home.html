How To Get Rid Of Cockroaches At Home

Cockroaches come at night and go in your food. They love dirty kitchens and wet places. Here is how to chase them away cheap.

1. Baking Soda And Sugar Trick

Mix 3 spoons baking soda and 3 spoons sugar. Put it in bottle caps in corners, under the sink and behind the fridge. Cockroaches eat it and die. Keep away from children and pets.

2. Keep Kitchen Clean

Never leave dirty dishes overnight. Wipe counters, take out rubbish every night, and keep food in closed containers. If there is no food, cockroaches will leave.

3. Close Holes

Close small holes near pipes with newspaper or cloth. Cockroaches come from drains. Pour boiling water in drains once a week.

Conclusion

Clean kitchen + baking soda trick = no cockroaches. Do it for 2 weeks and you will see the difference.
