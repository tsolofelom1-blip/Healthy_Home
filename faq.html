Questions Everyone Asks Us ❓

Here are the answers to the questions we get most from Mzansi families. If you don't find your answer here, contact us.

1. Is vinegar safe to clean with?

Yes! Vinegar mixed with water is safe for most surfaces - tiles, glass, counters, floors. Don't use it on marble or granite. It's cheap, it kills germs, and it's safe around kids and pets.

2. How do I get rid of cockroaches permanently?

Use the baking soda + sugar trick from our article. Put it in corners every night for 7 days. Also keep your kitchen clean and take out rubbish every night. Cockroaches come for food - no food = no cockroaches.

3. How do I remove mould from walls?

Spray vinegar directly on the mould, leave for 1 hour, then scrub with an old toothbrush. Open windows every day to stop mould coming back. Mould loves damp, dark rooms.

4. How can I save water and electricity at home?

Short showers, fix leaks, wash full loads only. For electricity: switch off geyser when not in use, use LED bulbs, unplug chargers. We have full guides on both - check our homepage.

5. Are your tips safe for babies and kids?

Yes. Most of our tips use vinegar, baking soda and lemon - not harsh chemicals. We always tell you when NOT to use a chemical around kids. Your family's safety is our first priority.

6. How often should I clean my house?

Kitchen every day, bathroom every 2-3 days, floors 2-3 times a week, deep clean once a week. Follow our Daily Cleaning Routine For Busy Moms - it only takes 30 minutes a day.

7. Do you sell cleaning products?

No, we don't sell anything. We just share free tips to help South African families save money and live healthier. All our articles are 100% free.

8. Can I share your tips with my friends?

Yes please! Share our website with your friends, family, church group, WhatsApp group - everyone. The more people who learn these tips, the healthier Mzansi becomes.
