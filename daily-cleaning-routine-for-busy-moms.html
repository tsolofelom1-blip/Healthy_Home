Daily Cleaning Routine For Busy Moms

Only 30 minutes a day, house always clean. You don't need to clean the whole house every day. If you do a little every day, your house never gets messy.

Morning - 10 Minutes

  • Make beds as soon as you wake up - house looks clean instantly
  • Open windows for fresh air, even 10 minutes helps
  • Wipe kitchen counter + wash breakfast dishes straight away
  • Take out rubbish bag if it's full

Afternoon - 10 Minutes

  • Pick up toys, shoes and clothes - everything back in its place
  • Sweep or mop kitchen floor - where most dirt is
  • Wipe toilet seat + sink quickly with disinfectant
  • Put one load of washing on

Evening - 10 Minutes

  • Wash dishes or load dishwasher - never sleep with dirty dishes
  • Wipe stove after cooking - oil is easy now, hard tomorrow
  • Pack school bags + lay out clothes for tomorrow
  • Spray bedroom with freshener or open window
Mzansi Tip: Get kids to help! Even 5-year-olds can pick up toys and make their bed. Make it a game - "who can finish first?" - and you save 10 minutes every day.

Weekly - Once A Week

Pick ONE day for deep clean: bathroom deep clean, fridge check, mop all floors. If you do the daily 30 minutes, the weekly clean is only 1 hour, not the whole day.

The Rule That Changes Everything

Don't put it down, put it away. If you touch it, put it back straight away. That one rule keeps your house clean without extra work.
