How To Clean Your Kitchen In 15 Minutes (Even If You Are Tired)

You don't need 2 hours to clean your kitchen. You just need a system. This is the 15-minute routine Mzansi moms use every night.

Minute 0-2: Clear Everything

Put all dirty dishes in the sink. Throw all rubbish in the bin. Put food back in the cupboard and fridge. Clear counters completely. A clear counter makes the kitchen look 50% cleaner already.

Minute 2-5: Wash Dishes Fast

Fill sink with hot water, vinegar and a little dishwashing liquid. Wash plates first, then pots. Don't scrub hard - let the vinegar do the work. Put clean dishes on the drying rack.

Minute 5-10: Wipe Everything

Spray your counters, stove and table with vinegar + water (50/50). Wipe with a clean cloth. Don't forget the handles of the fridge, cupboard doors, and light switches - those are the dirtiest places.

Minute 10-13: Floor

Sweep quickly. Then mop with hot water + a splash of vinegar. If you have tiles, this makes them shine without expensive floor cleaner.

Minute 13-15: Finishing Touch

Take out the rubbish bag and put a new one. Put a fresh dishcloth out. Boil water with lemon peel for 2 minutes - your kitchen will smell amazing and fresh for tomorrow morning.

Secret: Clean your kitchen EVERY night before bed. If you wake up to a clean kitchen, your whole day feels easier and happier.

Clean home. Healthy family. Small budget.
