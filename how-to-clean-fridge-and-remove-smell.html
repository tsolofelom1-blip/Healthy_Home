How To Clean Fridge And Remove Smell

No more bad smell in your fridge. Bad smell comes from old food and spills. In 20 minutes you can fix it with baking soda and vinegar.

1. Empty It

  • Take everything out. Throw away expired food and anything that smells off.
  • Put frozen food in a cooler bag or bucket while you clean.

2. Wash Shelves

  • Remove shelves and drawers. Wash with hot water + dish soap. Dry well.
  • Wipe inside of fridge with 1 litre warm water + 2 tablespoons baking soda.

3. Kill The Smell

  • Put an open box of baking soda or a cup of vinegar inside — it absorbs smell.
  • Put a lemon half inside for fresh smell.
  • Never put strong chemicals — food will taste like it.
Mzansi Tip: With load shedding, keep fridge closed during power cuts. After power returns, check milk and meat first. If meat smells sour, throw it — don't risk it.

Keep It Fresh

Check fridge every Sunday. Wipe spills same day. Keep leftovers in sealed containers with dates.
