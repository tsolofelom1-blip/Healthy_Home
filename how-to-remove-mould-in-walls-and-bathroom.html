How To Remove Mould From Walls And Bathroom

Do you have black spots on your bathroom walls or in the corners of your bedroom? That is mould. It is very common in South African homes in winter, especially in Emalahleni, Soweto, Durban and Cape Town where there is a lot of damp. Mould is not just ugly, it can make your children cough and sneeze. The good news is you can remove it for less than R40 with things from Shoprite or Boxer.

What You Need

You need white vinegar, baking soda, an old toothbrush, a spray bottle, gloves, and a cloth. Do NOT use bleach only, because bleach makes the mould look white but it comes back after 2 weeks. Vinegar kills the mould from the root.

Step 1: Spray Vinegar

Fill your spray bottle with pure white vinegar, do not mix with water. Spray the mould until it is very wet. Leave it for 1 hour. Do not touch it yet. Open your windows so the house gets air. Wear your gloves and a mask if you have one.

Step 2: Scrub With Baking Soda

Mix 2 spoons of baking soda with a little water to make a paste. Put the paste on the mould and scrub with your toothbrush. You will see the black spots coming off. For tiles and bathroom, scrub hard in the corners where mould hides. Wipe with a clean damp cloth.

Step 3: Spray Again And Leave

Spray vinegar one more time and leave it to dry. Do NOT rinse it. This stops the mould from coming back quickly. If you rinse it, the mould will return in 1 week.

How To Stop Mould From Coming Back

1. Open your bathroom window every day after you bath. 2. Don't dry clothes inside the bedroom. 3. Wipe wet walls with a dry cloth. 4. If your roof leaks, fix it quickly. Mould loves wet, closed rooms. If you keep the room dry and open, mould cannot grow.

When To Call Someone

If the mould covers a whole wall, or if your child has asthma and coughs a lot, don't try to clean it alone. Call a professional or ask your landlord to fix the damp problem. Your health is more important than saving money.

Conclusion

You don't need expensive sprays to remove mould. Vinegar and baking soda work better and cost less than R40. Try this today and your bathroom will be clean and safe again. Share this with your family.
