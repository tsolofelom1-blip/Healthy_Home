How To Keep Your Home Safe For Kids And Babies

Your home can be dangerous for a small child, even if it looks safe to you. Babies put everything in their mouths and toddlers love to climb. Here is how to make your home safe for less than R100.

1. Kitchen - The Most Dangerous Room

Keep knives, cleaning products, matches and plastic bags high up where kids cannot reach. Never leave a pot handle sticking out - a child can pull it down and burn themselves. Put cleaning products like bleach and Domestos in a high cupboard, NOT under the sink. Use a lock or even a piece of rope to keep cupboard doors closed.

2. Bathroom - Drowning Can Happen In 2cm Of Water

Never leave a baby alone in the bath, not even for 5 seconds to fetch a towel. Empty buckets after cleaning - a baby can fall head-first into a bucket and drown. Keep medicines, razors and cleaning products in a locked box. Put a non-slip mat in the bath so toddlers don't slip.

3. Electricity And Plugs

Cover all plug points with plug covers - they cost less than R20 for a pack at Pep or China shop. Keep phone chargers and cables away from babies - they chew them. Don't let kids play near a heater, iron, or kettle cord. If you use a paraffin heater, keep it far from curtains and beds.

4. Floors And Sharp Corners

Put old socks or sponge on sharp table corners. Keep small things like coins, batteries, beads and bottle caps off the floor - babies choke on them. Sweep every day. If you have tiles, wipe spills immediately so no one slips.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Keep the poison number on your fridge: 0861 555 777 (Poisons Information Centre). If your child drinks cleaning product, do NOT make them vomit. Take them to clinic immediately with the bottle.

5. Safe Cleaning For Homes With Babies

Don't use strong chemicals like bleach when babies are crawling. Use vinegar + water instead - it's safe if a baby touches the floor. Don't spray Doom or insect spray when kids are in the room. Wait 30 minutes and open windows before they come back.

Quick Safety Checklist

✅ Cleaning products up high
✅ Plug covers on
✅ Buckets emptied
✅ Small objects off floor
✅ Medicines locked away
✅ Poison number on fridge
