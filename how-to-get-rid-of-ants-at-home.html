How To Get Rid Of Ants At Home

Stop ants in 1 day with this cheap trick. Ants come inside in South Africa because they are looking for sugar, water and food crumbs. If you remove the food and block their path, they leave fast.

1. Clean The Trail First

Ants follow a smell trail. Wipe the counter, floor and cupboards with hot water + vinegar or lemon. This erases the trail so new ants get lost.

2. The Cheap Ant Trick That Works

  • Mix 1 tablespoon sugar + 1 tablespoon baking soda + a little water to make a paste
  • Put small drops on cardboard near where ants walk — NOT where kids or pets can reach
  • Ants carry it back to the nest. In 1-2 days, they are gone
Mzansi Tip: Don't kill every ant you see. Let them carry the mixture back. If you spray them all, the nest survives.

3. Block Where They Enter

  • Seal cracks around windows and doors with petroleum jelly or silicone
  • Put bay leaves or cinnamon at the door — ants hate the smell
  • Keep sugar, honey and dog food in sealed containers

4. Keep Them Away For Good

Wipe counters every night, take out rubbish, and mop with vinegar water twice a week. No food = no ants.
