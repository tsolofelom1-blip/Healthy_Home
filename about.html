Who Are We?

Healthy Home SA was started in Emalahleni, Mpumalanga by Tsolofelo. The idea is simple: Many South African families want a clean, healthy home but they don't have R1000s to spend on expensive cleaning products, aircons, and renovations.

We test cheap, real methods that work in real SA homes - RDP houses, flats, backyard rooms, and township homes. We use vinegar, baking soda, sunlight, and smart habits.

What We Do

We write simple guides on how to clean, remove mould, get rid of cockroaches, save water and electricity, and keep your family safe. No big words, no expensive products.

Our Promise

Everything we recommend is cheap, safe, and tested. If it doesn't work for a normal South African home, we won't post it.
