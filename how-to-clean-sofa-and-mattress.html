How To Clean Sofa And Mattress

Remove smells and stains from sofa & mattress. You don't need a machine. Baking soda + vinegar does most of the work.

1. Sofa Cleaning

  • Vacuum all crumbs, dust and hair first.
  • Mix warm water + 1 teaspoon dish soap. Dampen cloth, wipe sofa gently. Don't soak it.
  • For stains, dab with vinegar water. Don't rub hard — it spreads the stain.

2. Mattress Cleaning

  • Strip bed. Vacuum mattress well.
  • Sprinkle baking soda over whole mattress. Leave 1 hour — it pulls out smell.
  • Vacuum again. Spray lightly with vinegar water and let dry in sun if possible.

3. Remove Pee / Baby Smell

  • Blot (don't rub) with towel. Sprinkle baking soda, leave 30 minutes.
  • Spray vinegar, let fizz, blot again. Dry with fan or sun.
Mzansi Tip: Put a waterproof mattress protector on after cleaning. It saves you R1000s. Wash it weekly. Sun your mattress once a month — sun kills germs for free.
