How To Clean Mattress And Remove Smell

Your mattress can smell bad from sweat and dust. You can clean it without washing it.

Steps

1. Take off bedding and vacuum mattress. 2. Sprinkle a lot of baking soda all over. 3. Leave for 1 hour, or overnight if smell is strong. 4. Vacuum again. 5. For stains, spray vinegar lightly and wipe. Let it dry in the sun if you can.

Tip

Do this once a month. Put mattress in the sun once a month to kill germs.
