How To Save Water At Home In South Africa

Water is expensive in South Africa, and sometimes it runs out. Here is how Mzansi families save water every day without changing their whole life.

From Cape Town droughts to Joburg water cuts, we all know water is precious. The good news is you don't need expensive gadgets to save water. You just need small daily habits.

1. In The Bathroom - Save The Most Water Here

Most water is wasted in the bathroom. Take a short shower of 2 minutes instead of a bath. A bath uses 80 litres, a short shower uses only 20 litres. Turn off the tap while you brush your teeth. Don't flush the toilet every time for just urine - save flushing for number 2.

2. In The Kitchen

Don't wash dishes under a running tap. Fill one basin with soapy water to wash, and one basin with clean water to rinse. Use the dirty dish water to water your plants or flush the toilet. If you boil eggs or veggies, let that water cool and use it for plants - it has good nutrients.

3. Fix Leaks Immediately

One dripping tap can waste 20 litres per day. That's 600 litres per month! Check your taps, toilet and outside pipes. If your toilet keeps running after you flush, the rubber inside is broken. It costs less than R50 to fix and saves you hundreds on your water bill.

Quick Tip: Put a 2-litre bottle filled with water inside your toilet cistern. Every flush will use 2 litres less, and you won't notice any difference.

4. Laundry - Wash Smarter

Only wash full loads. Don't wash 2 shirts alone. Use the short cycle if your clothes are not very dirty. Reuse the grey water from your washing machine to clean your floors or yard.

5. Outside The House

Don't use a hosepipe to wash your car or driveway. Use a bucket and cloth. Water your garden early in the morning or late in the evening, not in the hot midday sun where water evaporates.

How Much Can You Save?

If a family of 4 follows these tips, they can save 5,000 to 10,000 litres per month. That's a big saving on your municipal bill and it helps South Africa during drought.
