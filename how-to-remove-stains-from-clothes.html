How To Remove Stains From Clothes

Remove curry, oil and blood stains easily. The secret is: treat it FAST, never rub with hot water first. Hot water sets the stain.

1. Curry / Turmeric Stains

  • Scrape off extra curry. Rinse with COLD water.
  • Rub with dishwashing liquid + lemon juice. Leave in sun for 30 minutes.
  • Wash normally. Sun fades turmeric naturally.

2. Oil / Fat Stains

  • Sprinkle baking soda or maize meal on oil. Leave 15 minutes to absorb.
  • Rub with dish soap, then wash in warm water.

3. Blood Stains

  • Always use COLD water. Hot water makes blood permanent.
  • Soak in cold salt water for 30 minutes, then wash with soap.
Mzansi Tip: For school uniforms, keep a small bottle of dish soap + vinegar. Treat stain the same day and it will come out. Old stains are 10x harder.

What NOT To Do

Don't put stained clothes in tumble dryer before stain is gone. Heat locks it in forever. Always check before drying.
