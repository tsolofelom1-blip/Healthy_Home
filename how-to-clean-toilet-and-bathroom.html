How To Clean Toilet And Bathroom

Make your bathroom shine like new. You don't need expensive chemicals. With vinegar, baking soda and 20 minutes, your toilet can look and smell fresh.

1. Start With The Toilet

  • Pour 1 cup vinegar inside the bowl + sprinkle baking soda. Leave 10 minutes.
  • Scrub with toilet brush, under the rim and at the bottom.
  • Flush, then wipe the seat, lid and handle with disinfectant.

2. Clean The Sink And Taps

Rub taps with lemon or vinegar to remove white water marks. Wipe sink with dish soap water. Rinse and dry for shine.

3. Tiles And Floor

  • Mop floor with hot water + small cap of bleach or vinegar.
  • Scrub grout lines with old toothbrush + baking soda paste.
  • Open window to dry — no damp = no mould.
Mzansi Tip: Clean toilet LAST with a separate cloth. Never use the same cloth for toilet and sink. Keep one cloth just for toilet.

How Often?

Toilet: every 2 days. Full bathroom: once a week. If you have kids, wipe toilet seat daily — it takes 1 minute.
