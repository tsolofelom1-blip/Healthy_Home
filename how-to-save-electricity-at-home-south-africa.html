How To Save Electricity At Home South Africa

Electricity is expensive. Prepaid runs out too fast. Here are 7 easy ways to save money every month.

1. Switch Off At The Plug

TV, charger and microwave use power even when off. Switch off at the wall.

2. Use LED Bulbs

LED bulbs use 80% less power than old bulbs. Change them one by one.

3. Boil Only What You Need

Don't fill the kettle to the top for one cup. Boil only 1 cup.

4. Wash With Cold Water

Wash clothes with cold water. It cleans the same and saves power.

5. Iron Once A Week

Iron all clothes at once instead of every day.

6. Geyser

Switch geyser off when you don't need hot water. Switch on 1 hour before bathing.

7. Use Sunlight

Open curtains in the day. Don't switch lights on if sun is enough.
